**Cc:** General Manager, Park Board  
**From:** [Your name / organization]  
**Re:** Permanent Jericho Beach court upgrades — surfaces, nets, and solar lighting pilot

Dear Commissioners,

I am writing to support permanent, high-quality court infrastructure at **Jericho Beach Park**.

Jericho is one of Vancouver's busiest outdoor court sites. Since 2023, two tennis courts have hosted eight pop-up pickleball courts. The Park Board has noted these remain **temporary** while a Sport Court Strategy is developed. In practice, players use these courts daily, yet the site still relies on portable nets, aging asphalt, and has **no lighting** for evening play.

**I respectfully request:**

1. **Permanent designation** of pickleball courts at Jericho, with fixed net systems and windscreens  
2. **Full resurfacing** of all four court slabs to Park Board quality standards  
3. **Preservation of tennis access** on remaining courts through clear multisport allocation  
4. **A solar LED lighting pilot** with directional fixtures, curfew hours, and neighbour consultation  
5. **A public timeline** for Sport Court Strategy implementation, with Jericho in the first tranche  

The Vancouver Pickleball Association's MORE campaign has documented city-wide demand: roughly 38 outdoor courts serving 680,000+ residents, with Jericho and Champlain among the most critical sites. Upgrading Jericho is a evidence-backed, west-side equity investment—not a new experiment.

I am happy to support this work through data collection, community outreach, or partnership discussions. I ask for a meeting with Park Board staff to review feasibility and capital planning options.

Thank you for your consideration.
