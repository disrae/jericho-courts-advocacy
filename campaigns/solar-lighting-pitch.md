# Solar court lighting — pitch appendix

## Why include lighting

- Jericho courts are unusable after ~8pm in winter—limits workers and seniors
- Lighting increases programming hours without a new indoor facility
- **Solar + battery** may avoid expensive grid trenching across park land

## Design principles (for staff memo)

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Directional fixtures** | Full cutoff LEDs, aimed downward onto playing surface only |
| **Curfew** | e.g. 8am–9pm default; adjustable seasonally |
| **Noise** | Lighting does not increase noise—pair with existing court etiquette signage |
| **Maintenance** | Panel cleaning 2×/year; battery replacement cycle documented |
| **Pilot scope** | 2–4 poles at Jericho south courts first |

## Policy alignment

- Park Board **asset needs** call for improved court sports quality and capacity
- City climate action — on-site renewable for public amenity
- Compare: other cities installing LED court lighting with timer controls

## Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Light spill to homes | Site survey, lower mounting, shields, curfew |
| Vandalism | Lockable equipment cages |
| Winter solar yield | Size battery for winter hours or hybrid grid backup |
| Cost overrun | Phase 1: lights on 2 courts only |

## Sample sentence for letters

"We request a **solar LED court lighting pilot** at Jericho with directional fixtures, timer curfew, and neighbour consultation— expanding safe play hours without significant grid infrastructure."
