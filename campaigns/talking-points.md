# Talking points — meetings & media

## One-liner

Jericho proves demand every day—it's time to match that with permanent courts, proper surfaces, and responsible solar lighting.

## 30-second version

Jericho Beach has eight pickleball courts on a temporary pop-up basis since 2022. They're full every day, but still use portable nets and old asphalt, with no lights. The Park Board says a Sport Court Strategy will decide the future—we've been waiting years. Jericho should be first in line: resurface the slabs, install permanent nets, keep tennis access, and pilot solar lighting with a curfew. Vancouver talks about active living; this is the cheapest high-impact win on the west side.

## Key facts

- Pop-ups extended **indefinitely** Feb 2023 — still **not permanent** ([City news](https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/vancouver-picklers-get-a-double-bounce-as-pop-up-court-project-is-extended.aspx))
- **8** pickleball courts on **2** converted tennis courts at Jericho
- **4** tennis courts total at site
- **No** court lighting today
- VPA asks Jericho + Champlain permanent by **spring 2026**
- ~**38** outdoor PB courts city-wide vs **680k+** residents (VPA)

## Answers to pushback

**"It's only temporary until the strategy."**  
Three+ years of daily use is a pilot with results. Delay has a cost: portable gear, repainting, frustrated players.

**"Tennis players will lose out."**  
Proposal keeps tennis courts and converts the already-dedicated pop-up slab—no surprise loss.

**"Lighting will bother neighbours."**  
Pilot with shielded LEDs, 9pm curfew, and consultation before install—learn from other municipalities.

**"Solar is gimmicky."**  
Solar reduces trenching/grid cost, supports Park Board climate goals, extends hours modestly without a new building.

## Ask (repeat at end)

Fund Jericho permanent courts in the next capital cycle—with resurfacing, fixed nets, and a solar lighting pilot.
