USA Pickleball suggests 30 × 60 ft (9.14 × 18.29 m) per court. This pad gives each of 8 courts 27.6 × 60.5 ft, so the length fits and the sides are 2.5 ft tight.

Pad measured from a 10 cm aerial photo and checked against the existing tennis lines (23.77 × 10.97 m, read as 23.8 × 11.0 m). Good to about ±0.3 m. A concept sketch, not a construction drawing.
