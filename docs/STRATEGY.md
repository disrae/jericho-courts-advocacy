# Strategy

## Problem statement

Jericho Beach courts are heavily used but run on **temporary** pickleball infrastructure (portable nets in lockboxes, tennis-net catch nets, pop-up designation). Surfaces need periodic repair/resurfacing. There is **no court lighting**. Demand exceeds capacity daily.

The Vancouver Park Board owns 177 tennis courts across 50 parks and is developing court-sport asset planning separately from the approved **Sport Field Strategy** (June 2024, fields-focused). Pickleball advocates have waited years for a **Sport Court Strategy** with no public implementation timeline.

## Theory of change

```
Evidence (usage data, condition, comparables)
    → Coalition (VPA, neighbours, tennis players, climate/solar allies)
    → Park Board staff buy-in (feasibility, cost, maintenance model)
    → Commissioner political will
    → Capital budget line item + delivery
```

Agents accelerate the **evidence** and **drafting** legs. Humans own relationships and public process.

### Phase 1 — Evidence (2–4 agent sprints)

**Usage & demand**
- Time-lapse or periodic observation log (manual + optional scrape of social/check-in proxies)
- Wait-time surveys (simple Google Form → export to `data/`)
- Compare Jericho utilization vs. other west-side courts

**Condition & cost**
- Document surface cracks, drainage, line quality (photo checklist in `data/photos/`)
- FOIL-style public records: past resurfacing contracts at Jericho (ConstructConnect / Park Board bids)
- Ballpark CAPEX/OPEX from comparable Vancouver projects + other BC municipalities

**Policy hook**
- Track Sport Court Strategy status (staff reports, Shape Your City, commissioner questions)
- Map capital planning cycle (when 2027–2031 capital plan opens)

### Phase 2 — Coalition & narrative

- Align with [VPA MORE campaign](https://vancouverpickleball.ca/more-campaign/) — avoid duplicating, offer data support
- Identify tennis vs. pickleball shared interests (2 courts remain tennis; multisport hub framing)
- West-side residents / Jericho Sailing Centre / beach users as amenity allies
- **Solar pitch:** tie to Park Board climate goals, off-grid lighting, extended usable hours without full grid trenching

## The ask (specific)

Draft Park Board request — refine with coalition:

1. **Designate Jericho south courts as permanent pickleball** (8 dedicated courts minimum at site)
2. **Resurface all 4 tennis court slabs** to Park Board standard (acrylic/plexipave class)
3. **Install permanent net systems** (fixed posts, pro-quality nets, windscreens)
4. **Pilot solar LED court lighting** with curfew + shielding to address neighbour concerns
5. **Publish Sport Court Strategy timeline** with Jericho in tranche 1

## Open questions (decide with user)

1. **Primary sport framing:** pickleball-led (VPA alignment) vs. multisport hub vs. tennis restoration?
2. **Scope:** upgrade in place vs. advocate for 12–16 court hub elsewhere in park?
3. **Solar:** nice-to-have or hard requirement? Any neighbour on record re: light spill?
4. **Coalition:** reach out to VPA officially or run parallel grassroots track?
5. **Budget angle:** pure Park Board capital vs. partnership / naming / grant (BC Active Communities)?

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Tennis/pickleball conflict | Multisport schedule + dedicated pickleball courts |
| Neighbour opposition (noise, lights) | Hours cap, directional LEDs, engagement early |
| “Strategy still in development” delay | FOIA records, commissioner questions, media |
| Temporary becomes permanent-enough | Frame pop-up fatigue + maintenance cost of portable gear |

## Success metrics

- Park Board written commitment with timeline
- Capital line item $ identified
- Construction start date
- Permanent nets installed
- Optional: lights operational with documented usage increase & zero major complaints
