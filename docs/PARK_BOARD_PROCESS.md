# Park Board Process — How to Win

## Capital planning cycle (typical)

1. **Asset needs documents** — court sports listed in [Parks & recreation asset needs](https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/parks-and-recreation-asset-needs.aspx)
2. **Strategy** — Sport Field Strategy approved 2024; **court-specific strategy** still referenced as in development since ~2018 per VPA
3. **Capital plan** — multi-year; projects need staff proposal + commissioner approval
4. **RFP / construction** — e.g. tennis court repair contracts (annual/multi-year)

**Agent task:** scrape meeting agendas for "court", "pickleball", "Jericho", "Sport Court" to catch staff reports.

## Engagement channels

### 1. Public meetings

- Agenda posted at [parkboardmeetings.vancouver.ca](https://parkboardmeetings.vancouver.ca/)
- Register to **depute** (3 min public comment) when relevant items on agenda
- Ask commissioners questions even on unrelated items (less effective but visible)

### 2. Shape Your City

- Monitor projects at [shapeyourcity.ca](https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/)
- Sport Field Strategy consultation **closed** — watch for court strategy engagement

### 3. Written correspondence

- Email commissioners + GM with concise ask, data appendix, coalition support
- Template: [`campaigns/letter-template-park-board.md`](../campaigns/letter-template-park-board.md)

### 4. Unsolicited proposals / partnerships

- Sport Field Strategy mentions fundraising & unsolicited proposals (Action 8.C)
- Solar vendor partnership may fit — needs legal/commercial review

### 5. Media

- Local: CBC, Global, Daily Hive, Vancouver Sun
- Hook: busiest courts in city still "temporary" after 3+ years

## FOIA / access to information

City of Vancouver FOI for:
- Jericho resurfacing history
- Sport Court Strategy draft timelines
- Maintenance costs portable vs. permanent nets
- Any lighting impact studies from other parks

## What moves the needle

1. **Commissioner champion** — identify who questions staff on courts
2. **Staff feasibility memo** — make it easy to say yes (cost, maintenance, design)
3. **Packed deputation room** — coordinated speakers
4. **Budget line** — named project in capital plan beats vague strategy promise

## Jericho-specific hooks

- West side underserved vs. east side pop-ups (Rupert, Strathcona)
- Site already has demand proof — lowest-risk permanent conversion candidate
- Solar lighting pilot = climate story + extended programming hours without new building
